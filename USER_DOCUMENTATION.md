## 1. Getting Started

### System Requirements
- Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer
- Internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
- Google account (optional, for Google Sign-In)

### Installing the App
1. Download the APK from the provided link **or** install from the Google Play Store
2. If installing from APK: go to **Settings → Security → Allow Unknown Sources**
3. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**
4. Once installed, tap **Open**

---

## 2. Creating an Account

1. On the **Login** screen, tap **"Don't have an account? Register"**
2. Fill in your details:
   - **Full Name** — your first and last name
   - **Email Address** — a valid email you have access to
   - **Phone Number** — your South African mobile number (e.g. 082 123 4567)
   - **Password** — minimum 8 characters
   - **Confirm Password** — must match your password exactly
3. Tap **Register**
4. Your account is created and you are taken to the Home screen

> 💡 **Tip:** Use a strong password with a mix of uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters (e.g. `Lesego@2024!`)

---

## 3. Signing In

### Option A — Email & Password
1. Enter your **email address** and **password**
2. Tap **Login**

### Option B — Google Sign-In (SSO)
1. Tap **"Continue with Google"**
2. Select your Google account from the list
3. You are signed in automatically — no password needed

### Forgot Password
1. Enter your email address in the email field
2. Tap **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link from Firebase
4. Follow the link to set a new password

## 5. Browsing Services

1. Tap **"Services"** in the bottom navigation bar (lightning bolt icon)
2. Use the **filter chips** at the top to narrow by category:
   - All · Residential · Commercial · Industrial · Solar · Maintenance
3. Scroll through the service cards — each shows:
   - Service name and description
   - Starting price in ZAR
   - **"Book This Service"** button
4. Tap **"Book This Service"** on any card to go straight to the booking wizard with that service pre-selected

## 6. Booking a Service

The booking wizard has **3 steps**:

### Step 1 — Select Your Service
- Choose from the dropdown list of available services
- If you came from a service card, it is already pre-selected
- Tap **Next**

### Step 2 — Your Details & Schedule
Fill in:
- **Full Name** (pre-filled from your profile)
- **Email Address** (pre-filled from your account)
- **Phone Number**
- **Service Address** — where the electrician should come to
- **Preferred Date** — tap the calendar icon to pick a date
- **Preferred Time** — tap the clock icon to pick a time
- **Additional Notes** — optional description of the problem
- Tap **Next**

### Step 3 — Confirm & Submit
- Review your booking details on the summary card
- Tap **"Confirm Booking"** to submit
- On success you will see a **green confirmation screen** with your **Booking Reference** (e.g. `LES-001`)
- A confirmation email is sent to your email address

> ⚠️ **Note:** Bookings are subject to technician availability. You will receive a call to confirm the appointment time.

## 7. Finding a Contractor

1. Tap **"Find Pro"** in the bottom navigation bar
2. Use the **Search bar** to search by contractor name or ECB license number
3. Use the **filter dropdowns** to narrow by:
   - **Area** (Johannesburg, Sandton, Pretoria, Midrand, etc.)
   - **Specialty** (Residential, Commercial, Solar, etc.)
4. Tap **Go** to apply filters
5. Each contractor card shows:
   - Name and specialty
   - Location and years of experience
   - Star rating and number of reviews
   - ECB license number
6. **"View Profile"** — see the contractor's full profile page
7. **"Hire Now"** — send a hire request directly to that contractor

## 8. My Bookings

View your booking history from your **Profile** screen:

1. Tap **"Profile"** in the bottom navigation (person icon)
2. The **My Bookings** card shows your total count and status summary
3. Booking statuses:
   - 🟡 **Pending** — submitted, awaiting confirmation
   - 🟢 **Confirmed** — appointment confirmed by our team
   - ✅ **Completed** — job done
   - ❌ **Cancelled** — booking was cancelled

> 📶 Bookings are stored **offline** — you can view your history even without internet.

## 12. Troubleshooting

### App won't connect to the internet
- Check that Wi-Fi or mobile data is enabled
- Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data
- Restart the app

### Login fails
- Double-check your email and password (no extra spaces)
- Tap **"Forgot Password?"** to reset your password
- Try Google Sign-In if you registered with Google

### Services page shows "No services found"
- Check your internet connection — services load from our API
- Pull down to refresh the list
- Cached services from your last visit still show offline

### Booking not going through
- Ensure all required fields are filled in (marked with an asterisk)
- Check your internet connection
- Try again after a few minutes

### App is slow or freezing
- Close and reopen the app
- Restart your phone
- Check available storage (app needs at least 50 MB free)
